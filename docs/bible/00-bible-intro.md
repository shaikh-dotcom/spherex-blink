# Film bible

The bible is the creative and technical spec of the film. Sections are attached to each patch prompt as listed in that patch's front matter (`bible:` and `appendix:`). Sections: 01 brief, 02 look, 03 timeline, 04 architecture and contract, 05 evidence rules, 06 scene specs, 07 copy, 08 camera and focus, 09 audio, 10 honesty, 11 source map. Appendix A holds the captured evidence values.
