# 06 - Scene specs

Frames are global. "Land on a beat" = a multiple of 15. Cue frames are in `CUES` (timeline.ts) and texts in `copy.ts`. Values in [brackets] come from evidence selectors (appendix A shows them).

## S0 Hook (0-299), no app (appOpacity 0), full-bleed `HookField`
Deep ink star field, about 140 stars of varying size, a very slow deterministic drift and twinkle (seeded PRNG). f0-59: pure stillness. f60: caption "Almost everything in the sky / stands still." f150 (beat): the field "blinks" to a second epoch: all stars identical, ONE dot has jumped; blink alternates every 5 frames from f150 to f209 (a 3 Hz flicker). f210: callout "One dot jumps" with a ring around the dot that draws on. f240: mono tag "WHAT A BLINK COMPARATOR SHOWS" and the label "Illustration". f270-299: calm hold with slow push, last frame ready to cut hard to S1 on f300. The jumping dot moves on a diagonal; amber for epoch one, cyan for epoch two during the flicker's ghost frames.

## S1 Title (300-599), app hidden then revealed
Ink stage gradient. f300: eyebrow (`brand.eyebrow`) fades up in small caps. f315: `Mark` draws its rings (stroke draw) beside the lockup "SPHEREx *Blink Comparator*" (KineticText, serif, `Blink Comparator` in italics and amber). f420: tagline two lines (`s1.tagline`). f510-569: quiet, slow push. f570-599: the app emerges behind the title (appOpacity 0 to 1), camera starts at `fullWindow`, title text lifts away upward. Last frame of S1 = first pose of S2.

## S2 Data (600-1079): "this is real data"
f600: caption `s2.caption`. Camera: header (600-689) -> skyImage (690) -> bandPlates (780) -> figure (960-1079). Viewer shows the Pallas field frame A. f690: stat numerals count up: [overview.realFields] `real fields` and [overview.realFrames] `real frames` (StatNumeral, mono). f720: header badge "Real data · SPHEREx QR2". f780: `s2.bandsTag`; rail.hot "band"; band plates highlight D1 then D2, D4, D5 in turn (the bands that exist in this field: [pallas.bands]); the viewer shows a frame of each band (colour changes). f960: caption `s2.captionPatch`; time bar position sweeps through the frames; epoch dates appear. f1040-1079: settle on pair A at zoom 1 (S3 entry).

## S3 Blink (1080-1679): "stars stay put, one dot jumps"
f1080 (beat): Badge `badges.real`. Camera: figure/skyImage with slow pushes. f1110: rail.hot "blink", cursor to "Blink A ⇄ B", click at f1140: rail.mode "blink", viewer.blinkPhase flickers (3 Hz, 10 frames per half: A then B). f1170: callout `s3.calloutStars` on a bright star. f1200: callout `s3.calloutJump` with a ring on the asteroid, using [selectTargetPosition(A)] and [selectTargetPosition(B)] (tile pixels, then screen); the ring snaps from the A position to the B position each flip. f1230: caption `s3.captionPluto`. f1290: overlay: rail.mode "overlay", viewer.overlayAB true: amber A and cyan B on top of each other; the asteroid shows as a coloured pair; callout `s3.calloutAmber` on the "A amber / B cyan" key in the viewer HUD. f1410: caption `s3.captionPair`; rail.hot "pair"; cursor clicks "Best pair"; the pair info shows A and B ([pair.a] and [pair.b]: dates in UTC, wavelengths, [daysApart] days apart). f1590: callout `s3.calloutWarn` (the app's own caution that stars change brightness with wavelength). f1650-1679: back to static A.

## S4 Inspect (1680-2219): "hunt blind, click the dot"
f1680: caption `s4.caption`. f1710: callout `s4.calloutBlind`; rail.hot "layers"; cursor unticks "Known objects": rail.showKnown false (any ring and track vanish). Camera to skyImage, push toward the mover at its A position. f1770: cursor arrives near the dot, clicks: selection appears at the click point; f1800: callout `s4.calloutSnap`: the selection ring slides to the centroid [pick.centroid]. Panel (inspect tab) reveals the selected-point card: [pick.raHms], [pick.decDms], galactic and ecliptic, band, UTC, wavelength, brightness [pick.flux] (marked as a lower limit if so). f1890: mover check verdict `moves` (verdictReveal rises): callout `s4.calloutMover`; the card is the only thing allowed to be zoomed above 2.0, for at most 60 frames. f2040: back to the figure. f2100: caption `s4.captionKnown`; cursor re-ticks "Known objects": rail.showKnown true: the amber ring, dashed track and label "(2) Pallas (known asteroid)" draw on around the dot. Hold, slow push, to f2219.

## S5 Check (2220-2699): "checked, not drawn"
f2220: caption `s5.caption`; tabs.tab "context" (tab switch with a short underline slide). Field card ("This field") reveals. f2310: callout `s5.calloutChecked`; the Reality check card reveals (validationReveal): Pallas found in [13 of 13] frames, rms offset [0.57 px = 3.5 arcsec]; on the figure, a faint cross at each predicted position per frame. f2460: caption `s5.captionFields`; the card's across-fields line: found in [148 of 150] frames in [16] asteroid fields. f2580: caption `s5.captionStrict`; the stricter check [107 of 150]; the card shows, in the app's words, that some asteroids move too little in their window to pass it. Do not hide this. f2640: "Compare with other eyes": referenceBlend rises to about 0.6 (an optical or infrared reference picture over the tile), credit visible. f2699: blend back to 0.

## S6 Planet X (2700-3419): "what would a distant planet do, and what does the tool say"
f2700: tabs.tab "planetx"; caption `s6.caption`; `ParallaxDiagram` overlay (Earth on its orbit, two lines of sight to a distant body, tiny apparent shift; label "Illustration"). f2820: pxLab reveals: slider distance 30 to 500 AU ([selectShift(500)]: [windowPx] px in the window); viewer draws the violet track "Planet X @ 500 AU (simulated)" (pxDrawAu 500). f2880: callout `s6.calloutShift`. f3000: callout `s6.calloutSix`: the output line shows [sixMonthPx] px over six months, x[sixMonthOverWindow]; the viewer switches to the six-month field (viewer.fieldId "pallas-6mo") with the three survey passes ([passes]). f3060: caption `s6.captionMeasure`; viewer back to "pallas"; cursor clicks the mover in frame A and presses Mark A, steps to frame B, clicks the mover, Mark B (markA, markB; positions from target positions). f3150: the measurement output: [measuredPx] px ([measuredArcsec] arcsec) in [daysApart] days; the fit line, in the app's words for kind "near": "Nearby object: asteroid or comet. It moved far more than any body beyond [dminAu] AU can through parallax (even at [dminAu] AU the shift would be [predictedAtDminArcsec]″)." f3200: callout `s6.calloutNear`. f3300: caption `s6.captionHonest`; the Solar system card (orrery) reveals with the violet dashed direction line ("a distant Planet X would lie somewhere along it"). f3419: ends with markA/markB cleared, pxDrawAu null.

## S7 Flag (3420-3779): "flag it, export it"
f3420: caption `s7.caption`; tabs.tab "inspect"; viewer.selection = pick. f3450: flag form reveals: category "asteroid" ([selectFlagCategories]); cursor clicks "Flag it". f3480: callout `s7.calloutCross`; flags.flagged 1; the match note, in the app's words: "This matches a known object: (2) Pallas. Good catch. It is catalogued, so it is not a new discovery." f3540: caption `s7.captionExport`; Export CSV and Export JSON buttons pulse; a CSV preview strip shows the header row [selectFlagColumns] and the one row built from evidence. f3660: caption `s7.captionState`; the flag-mode pill and status line say where flags go. The deployed app has no shared store configured (web/js/config.js has an empty projectId), so the real wording is the pill "saved in this browser only" and the status "Saved in this browser (no shared store is configured)." Show exactly that; never imply flags are shared with everyone. f3779: settle.

## S8 Limits (3780-4199): "what is real, simulated, limited"
f3780: caption `s8.caption`; appOpacity eases to 0.15 under the `LimitsCard` overlay. f3810: three columns appear: REAL ([realFields] real fields, [realFrames] real frames, QR2; reality check 148 of 150), SIMULATED (one practice field, always labelled), LIMITED (2 to 6 of six detectors per field; a sample of the sky not the survey; stricter check 107 of 150 and why; Ceres 0 of 8 not yet explained). Rows appear one by one on beats. f3900: caption `s8.captionHatch` with a real tile showing the hatch for no coverage next to the word "no data". f4080: caption `s8.captionClaim`; Badge `badges.prototype`. f4170-4199: columns lift away.
